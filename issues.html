Southwest's biggest risks are the ones it controls.

Every theme in Southwest's news coverage, scored for how much it matters and ranked into a priority list, with the outlets and voices that drive each one.

What we found

Safety is the top-priority issue: 16% of coverage, 67% of it negative.

Policy changes are the biggest issue (24% of coverage) and land worse than tone scores suggest: 39% negative by headline review, against 9% by GDELT tone. Loyalty coverage is rising fast after the Rapid Rewards overhaul.

What it means

Most of Southwest's exposure is in issues it controls (policies, loyalty) or can prepare for (incidents). That makes it manageable.

Local TV and radio carry 41% of Southwest coverage, and CEO Bob Jordan is by far the most-named voice. That is where to respond, and who should do it.

What we recommend

Act now on safety response and on how policy changes are explained.

Contain regulation, labour and disruption stories with prepared positions.

Get ahead of loyalty: explain the Rapid Rewards changes before customers do.

Analyst note · how we prioritise

Priority score = reach × (negativity + severity) ÷ 2, scaled so the top issue = 100.

Severity is a stated judgement a client can change: safety and bankruptcy 3; regulation, labour, disruptions and passenger behaviour 2; fares, policies, loyalty and leadership 1; financial, network and marketing 0.

Momentum compares Aug–Nov coverage with the 12-month average: above 1.2× is rising, below 0.8× is easing.
