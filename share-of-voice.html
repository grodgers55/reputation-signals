Briefing 03Share of voiceProof of concept · public data

Cava's customers are searching. Its news is talking to investors.

Two fast-growing chains compared on how much they are talked about, in what tone and about what. The briefing is written as if Cava were the client: the challenger brand looking for room to grow.

What we found

Dutch Bros owns more of the conversation, but Cava's search interest grew 13% while Dutch Bros' was flat.

Dutch Bros has 58% of the pair's news coverage and 62% of search interest. Neither brand reached the Watch level on the risk index all year (Cava came closest, in August).

What it means

Cava's consumer momentum isn't reaching the news. 61% of its coverage is financial, written for investors, and a new Cava restaurant gets far less local coverage than a new Dutch Bros drive-thru (15% of coverage vs 27%).

With no reputational fire to fight, this is an offensive opportunity, not a defensive one.

What we recommend

Make every opening a local media event, the way Dutch Bros' openings are covered.

Take menu launches to consumer and local press, not only the earnings call.

Track share of voice monthly against the opening calendar to see what works.
